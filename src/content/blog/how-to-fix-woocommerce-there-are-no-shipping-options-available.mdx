So you have your WooCommerce setup and products added but then your customer is about to pay/checkout their items. WooCommerce for some reason decided to show your client “There are no shipping options available. Please ensure that your address has been entered correctly, or contact us if you need any help.” and cant checkout. Heres is the usual reason why that happens:

<ol class="not-prose mt-10 border-t border-l border-white/15">
  <ReasonSolution reason="Products have no defined weight.">Edit your product and add in weight</ReasonSolution>
  <ReasonSolution reason="No shipping method configured">Go to your backend dashboard. <code>WooCommerce &gt;&gt; Settings &gt;&gt; Shipping</code> and click Add Shipping Zone</ReasonSolution>
  <ReasonSolution reason="Wrong / Invalid Address">Check google maps if that address really exists and email customer back.</ReasonSolution>
</ol>
